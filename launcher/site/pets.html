Reading the table

  • Hover an ability for what it does (numbers are for its strongest rank). Italic ones are passive.
  • Elite creatures are tougher; Exotic ones need the ability to tame exotic pets.
  • "Tame one" is one example: the lowest-level ordinary creature of that family out in the world. Most families have several; the number after it says how many kinds can be tamed.
  • Each kind needs its own taming spell (Tame Beast, Tame Undead and so on). This page reads the live server, so it follows patches by itself.
