Reading the page

  • New is the race's signature passive, made for Wildcard. Its tooltip lists the buffs you will see while it works.
  • Changed is a stock racial that works differently here; the tooltip says how.
  • In game, type .racial to see what your signature passive is doing right now.
  • Numbers are for a level 60 character. The tooltips are the game's own, read from the server each night.
