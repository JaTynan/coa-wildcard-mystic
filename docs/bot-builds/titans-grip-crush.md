# Bot build: Titan's Grip Crush (melee)

Status: draft, 28 Nov 2026. Melee 4 of 6 (`raid-coverage.md`). Format and rules as in `plague-caster.md`. Nothing here
is live. Matches the bot module's named build **Heavyweight** (Heavyweight and Prepared to Crush are its talents).

Role: melee damage, strong on groups (its Crush hits are not target capped) and good single target. The raid's warrior:
it carries Battle Shout, the major armour debuff and the physical damage debuff.

**Source warning.** Season 9 guide (May 2024) on the classless realm, so possibly a free-pick build rather than
Wildcard. The author keeps a public spreadsheet.

## 2. How the build works

- **Titan's Grip**: a two-hander in each hand.
- **Heavyweight** and **Prepared to Crush!**: a Crush frontal that hits every enemy in front, triggered when there is
  no white swing to spend (an off-hand miss, or global cooldowns filling the swing time). **Gurubashi Berserker** shortens
  the warrior global cooldown to make that more frequent; **Hateful Strike** fills between globals.
- **Titan's Fury**: Titanic Strike spends all rage (up to 500 with the rage talent) for about 25% more damage with
  Titan's Grip, for 20 seconds. Keep it up.
- **Enrage** and **Raging Blow** charges are the single-target engine; **Raging Lunge** is the group version.
- **Blades of Blood** spreads Rend to groups, keeping **Hunger for Blood** (+10%) up.
- **Battle Fatigue** with **Echoing Warcry**: 10% more physical damage most of the time; the war cry adds crit.
- **Demolisher** with Ready to Demolish! and Expose Armor: a large armour penetration window every 20 seconds.
