# Faction rosters: which builds go Horde, which go Alliance

Status: draft, 28 Nov 2026. James's ask (28 Nov): more builds than the minimum, so Horde and Alliance field different
sets, with each build placed on the side whose race fits it best. This file places the 27 drafted builds, shows each
faction's 25-player raid, and lists the new builds needed to make the two sides different.

Races: the ten stock races (the bot module allows races 1-11 except goblin; custom races only if switched on). Each
race has its stock 3.3.5 racials plus our signature passive (`docs/race-kits.md`).

## 1. What each race is good for

| Race | Side | Signature passive (ours) | Stock racials that matter | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Human | A | Spellsword: raises the weaker of physical and spell power, crit and hit towards the stronger (little for builds that already convert) | +3% spirit; sword and mace expertise; Every Man for Himself | Spirit stackers (both spirit guides recommend human), hybrids without a conversion talent, sword or mace melee |
| Dwarf | A | Prospector's Spirit: gathering buffs (outside dungeons) | Stoneform (armour, removes bleeds and poisons); gun crit; mace expertise | Tanks, gun users, mace melee |
| Night Elf | A | Ambush: entering stealth readies the next ability (+50%) | Shadowmeld (stealth in combat); dodge | Stealth openers (rogue, cat), anyone who can Shadowmeld into a big hit |
| Gnome | A | Overclock: bigger resource pools, cheaper abilities, 10% resource a minute in combat | Escape Artist; dagger and one-hand sword expertise | Mana- or energy-starved builds; a tank whose mana is its health |
| Draenei | A | Radiant Presence: up to +5% damage and healing and 5% less damage taken for everyone within 30 yards; auras and totems reach 50% further | Heroic Presence (+1% hit for the group); Gift of the Naaru | Group builds: aura and totem carriers, healers, group tanks |
| Orc | H | Bloodrush: +2% damage per enemy fighting you (up to 10%); kill streaks give speed and haste | Blood Fury (attack and spell power); +5% pet damage; axe expertise; shorter stuns | Area melee, pet builds, burst openers |
| Undead | H | Grave Defiance: more damage and healing below 35% health; rise as a ghoul once | Will of the Forsaken (fear, charm, sleep); Cannibalize | Shadow builds, death knights, warlocks, risk-taking melee |
| Tauren | H | Plainsrunning: run speed builds to +100%; the group runs with you; Juggernaut tiers | +5% health; War Stomp | Tanks, druids, melee that charges in |
| Troll | H | Voodoo Regeneration: health regeneration in combat and a burst heal below 35% | Berserking (haste, more when hurt); bow and throwing crit | Haste-hungry casters and healers, thrown weapons, tanks that self-sustain |
| Blood Elf | H | Arcane Hunger: up to +15% damage and healing at +20% cost; Arcane Torrent refunds resources | Arcane Torrent (silence) | Arcane and holy casters, paladins, steady casters with mana to spare |
