## 2. How the build works (as S1 plays it)

- **Glacial Burst** is the frost version of Lava Burst and the biggest hitter; it needs **Frostflame Shock** on the
  target to crit (it uses Flame Shock modifiers).
- **Glacial Spike** is the big single hit, ramped by Water Bolts and icicles first.
- A water elemental, Ray of Frost and frost damage-over-time fill in.
- Groups: Frozen Orb and Frostfall, then spam the frost area spell, Deep Freeze whenever ready, Cone of Cold after Deep
  Freeze (it hits harder).
- Problems S1 hit: mana ran out in long fights, not hit capped, too many buttons.
