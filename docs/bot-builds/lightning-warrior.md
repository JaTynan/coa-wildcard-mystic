## 2. How the build works

- A warrior that hits with lightning: **Lightning Blade**, **Thunder Slam** (uses Slam modifiers) and **Thunder Clap**
  (a talent makes it scale with spell power).
- **Assault and Battery**: shoots lightning bolts; it shortens Chain Lightning's cooldown but only raises **Energy
  Bolt**'s damage, so Chain Lightning is for groups only.
- **Shamanistic Rage** keeps mana up; **Fel Infused Weapon** adds damage; Mental Quickness for the hybrid.
