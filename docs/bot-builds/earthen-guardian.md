# Bot build: Earthen Guardian, shaman tank (tank)

Status: draft, 28 Nov 2026. Tank 3 of 3 (`raid-coverage.md`). Format and rules as in `plague-caster.md`. Nothing here is
live.

Role: tank, very strong threat on groups from Fire Nova spam, and high damage for a tank (S1 was top damage in his
dungeons). Carries Stoneskin Totem for the raid (its own totems are fire totems, so the earth slot is free).

**Source warning.** The guide is for Ascension's Classic+ realm (Bronzebeard, February 2025), where a shaman is a fixed
class with "enchants" and artifact abilities, not a Wildcard Hero. The abilities all exist in our data and a Wildcard
Hero can roll them, so the build carries over, but the enchant numbers may differ.

## 2. How the build works

- **Earthen Guardian** (legendary enchant) gives **Stone Shield**, worn until cancelled: 15% less damage and 80% more
  threat. While it is up, **Primal Strike** raises an **Earthen Spike** under the target: high-threat damage to the
  target and every enemy near it (not target capped), and it returns 20% of base mana. Use it as often as possible.
- **Totemic Bond** (enchant): threat from Searing and Magma Totem damage goes to the shaman, so the totems are not
  attacked and **Fire Nova** can be spammed safely.
- **Fire Nova** with **Improved Fire Nova** and the **Glyph of Fire Nova**: a cooldown of about 3 seconds. Most of the
  build's group threat and damage.
- **Sundering** with **Cataclysmic Sundering**: a line of fire that leaves burning lava, plus a short incapacitate.
- **Windfury Weapon** with **Zephyr**: attack speed stacks and extra Windfury hits.
- Mana is the weak point (S1): keep **Water Shield** up; Primal Strike is the main mana return.
