# Bot build: Moonkin Fire (caster)

Status: draft, 28 Nov 2026. Caster 8 of 8 (`raid-coverage.md`). Format and rules as in `plague-caster.md`. Nothing here
is live.

Role: ranged damage and, mainly, **raid support**: it carries Moonkin Form (spell crit aura), Improved Scorch (the 5%
spell crit debuff), Wrath of Air and Mana Spring totems, and Blessing of Kings. Damage is middling.

**Source warning.** A Season 10 Wildcard leveling build whose own author calls it basic and boring. It is in the roster
for the buffs it carries, not its damage. The fire-caster rotation is the common one (Scorch, Fireball, instant Pyroblast
after two fire crits); see `pyroblast-fire-mage.md` for a stronger fire mage.

## 2. How the build works

- A fire caster in **Moonkin Form**: Fireball, Fire Blast, Scorch (castable while moving, applies Improved Scorch),
  Pyroblast made instant by two non-periodic fire crits, Ignite.
- **Earthquake** with a talent: enemies standing in it take extra damage from cast fire spells.
- Support: Wrath of Air and Mana Spring totems, Blessing of Kings, Innervate (own mana), Counterspell.
