# Race kits: first balance pass (13 November 2026)

A paper review of every racial in [race-kits.md](race-kits.md), assuming each works as written, before players
have tried them. Each ability is rated against four things:

- **Blizzard's budget.** In Wrath, each race had one strong active on a 2-3 minute cooldown (Will of the
  Forsaken, Every Man for Himself, Stoneform, War Stomp...) and a few small passives worth about 1-3% (1% hit,
  5% base health, a weapon skill). Our kits go past that on purpose: each race has a signature passive as
  noticeable as Plainsrunning. The rule kept here: **behaviour can be big; flat always-on damage or healing
  should stay near 5% at most**, because flat numbers are what decide min-max race picks.
- **Raw numbers:** what the effect is worth when it works.
- **PvP:** duels, world PvP and battlegrounds.
- **PvE and everything else:** levelling solo, dungeons, raids, travel, trade skills.

Tiers: **S** decides fights or race choice on its own, **A** strong, **B** solid, **C** minor, **D** weak or broken.

## Recommended tweaks

Ordered by impact. Numbers are starting points for player feedback.

1. **Set the missing cooldowns:** Grudge 2 min, Naaru's Ward 2 min, Command 2 min, Bull Rush 1.5 min, Eureka 2 min, Rapid
   Regeneration 3 min, Mana Tap 30 sec per charge (charges last 10 min).
2. **Plainsrunning in combat:** half the cap while in combat (+50% at 60), the full cap out of combat. Travel stays the
   same; chases in PvP stop being one-sided.
3. **Blood Fury:** +10% to start, then +2.5% per nearby ally up to 4 (+20% at most). Solo Orcs get something; the group peak
   is unchanged.
4. **Troll sustain:** Rapid Regeneration to a 3 min cooldown (above), and Voodoo Regeneration shares a 30 sec lockout
   with it, so both heals can't land back to back.
5. **Human Will to Survive:** 2 min, shared with the PvP trinket, as Wrath had it; drop the 10% damage reduction and
   keep the immunity.
6. **Human Regroup, new effect:** gain both Spellsword haste effects (attack and cast speed) at once for 8 sec. 2 min
   cooldown. It fits the hybrid theme and stays away from Will to Survive.
7. **Arcane Hunger:** +1.5% per charge (+4.5% at 3), within the 5% rule.
8. **Night Elf Ambush:** +50% against monsters, +30% against players. The opener stays the Night Elf's thing without
   one-shots.
9. **Gnome Eureka:** 12 sec instead of 5. Gnomes come up to the middle.
10. **Orc Forge Hardened:** instead of a 5% chance, the first killing blow every 3 min leaves you at 1 health instead.
    Plannable, still rare, and it doesn't step on Undead (who rise after death rather than not dying).

Left alone: Dwarf, Undead, Draenei (low alone, but fair for a group race), and the trade-skill passives.
